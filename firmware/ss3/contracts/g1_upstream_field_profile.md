# G1 compatibility profile

Copyright (c) 2026 Jonathan Yantis. MIT.

The selected receiver accepts the authenticated G1/GR3/RC7 layouts described by
the accompanying codec sources and public vectors. Group, key epoch and device
identity are supplied through ignored local enrollment. No fixed public roster
is required. This public successor omits the historical private bench roster.
Source permission is the original signed, matched correction permission;
locator discovery, HOLD presentation and relay leases cannot renew it.
