\begin{abstract}
Passive Precision Time over BLE (PToB) transfers shared time to heterogeneous Bluetooth Low Energy receivers. Periodic advertisements identify physical transmissions; later corrections let receivers discipline local clocks without a transaction for each timestamp. This release candidate pins the implemented BLE wire profile: G7V4 grandmaster control, GR3 root and RC7/profile 5 relay timing, JOIN5 and revision 8 relay admission, and coarse civil-time metadata. It defines when a source may serve, when a HOT successor may take over, and when a receiver must withdraw time. Byte compatibility, physical failover, and calibrated output accuracy are separate review gates.
\end{abstract}
\section{Purpose, scope and roles}
The service lets an endpoint identify a time source, maintain a local clock between observations, report provenance and withdraw unavailable time. Receivers may range from coarse callback consumers to hardware timestamping instruments. Packet resolution alone makes no accuracy promise.

This is an author review of the release candidate for the BLE service and profile. \must, \mustnot\ and \should\ express candidate requirements. The reference is the T096 0.15.326 source snapshot identified in the edition manifest. Its GM profile is \code{GM7v4+S2+C1+TZ1}: G7V4 wire, ordinary broadcast following, full mesh birth and authenticated time-zone metadata. G1 names the timing engine; GR3 names the selected root wire. V14 identifies the service edition, independent of wire revision and firmware version \cite{gm7,tz,wire}.

A device's role, functional clock availability and precision quality \must\ remain separately observable. Source transitions fence authority, captures and maps before replacement service. This BLE candidate leaves LoRa/RC8, connected RC9 bridging, and USB or wired time delivery to separate contracts. It is pending public review, with no SIG submission or firmware release implied.

The time scale is a POSIX UTC estimate with an authenticated source-coordinate mapping. Leap/TAI handling and wired PTP/gPTP translation need separate scale, delay and uncertainty contracts. Civil time never replaces UTC event identity.

\section{GM7 election, following and failover}
GM control uses authenticated extended advertisements. ANNOUNCE (ANN) names the ACTIVE source and its nominee, if any. A following GM listens to the selected source's GR3 train while its own GNSS/PPS continues. It reports independent eligibility and actual shadow preparation in STATUS; a qualified nominee echoes HOT in ordinary STATUS. GM following needs no JOIN, GATT, nonce or ANN-based receive lease. An ACTIVE GM still uses JOIN for ordinary clients \cite{gm7}.

Eligibility precedes ranking. The reference source requires 60 seconds of continuous independent GNSS/PPS qualification. Ordinary initial election then requires 30 seconds of quiet observation plus deterministic backoff up to 500 ms: a healthy cold start normally takes about 90 seconds plus backoff. A GM lacking its own qualified source may follow time but \mustnot\ originate it. Rank eligible candidates lexicographically by priority1, clock class, accuracy, variance, priority2 and stable identity, with boot identity resolving incarnation ties. Preference alone is never HOT confirmation.

HOT readiness requires a current same-scope delivered shadow map and fit, live correction/source evidence, qualified own GNSS/PPS and a physical phase enclosure within the 2 ms scheduling policy. Strict precision LOCK is a separate assessment. Nomination, diagnostics and displayed RMS cannot substitute for those readiness predicates.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{0.22\linewidth}L{0.70\linewidth}@{}}\toprule
Transition & Required behavior\\\midrule
Selected GM silence & Only the designated, still-qualified HOT uses the fast path: trigger at 2.5 s after last advancing selected-GM communication; target first completed G1 within 3 s of that endpoint. Actual capture, drain, source and reservation gates still apply.\\
Explicit YIELD & Stop-before-start at the immutable boundary, 750 ms after the original source sample; translate it through the retained physical map. Three copies 250 ms apart cannot move it.\\
WITHDRAW / source fault & Stop positive output immediately; attempt three immutable withdrawal copies within their original 1 s negative lifetime. A successor still needs its own authority and source readiness.\\
No usable HOT & An independently qualified COLD GM uses ordinary 30 s quiet election plus backoff. Held remote time cannot create source eligibility.\\

\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
GM following continues across control loss. Actual matched corrections, fit freshness and original source expiry bound delivered time. The receiver converts the correction's remaining grant to its captured RX coordinate, rounding down. An advancing selected direct header can establish liveness before a match is usable; it cannot train the clock. A remembered listening identity grants no map. The 3 s objective requires physical qualification.

\subsection*{Mesh lineage and convergence}
At first valid serving reservation, sample full 64-bit functional UTC microseconds as mesh birth and record the founder. Commit ACTIVE only after successful bounded ANN transmission; track actual RF completion separately. A possibly exposed claim retains its lineage even if completion fails. Successors inherit birth/founder and advance the leader term; boot, source epoch, capture generation and train remain separate scopes \cite{birth}.

When ACTIVE meshes meet, earlier birth wins; exact-birth ties use founder, then term/identity/boot ordering. The newer mesh stops positive output before following. The older ACTIVE keeps its service and nominee. Disconnected partitions can serve separate independently eligible sources; convergence does not promise consensus across absent links. Birth is a functional ordering value, not proof of calibrated UTC.

\section{Periodic timing and relay regeneration}
Timing uses LE periodic advertising, with complete application records at a nominal 62.5 ms interval (16 Hz). Acquisition uses extended-advertising discovery; synchronization is distinct from connecting. This uses periodic advertising without responses \cite{blecore}. An application record may span controller reports or link-layer fragments, so adapters \must\ deliver bounded whole records and preserve their event identity.

An original publication identifies a physical transmission without predicting its own future TX timestamp. A later record carries zero, one or two corrections naming earlier publications, periodic events and physical data channels (0--36). The receiver matches a correction to the saved physical RX capture of that exact original. Callback arrival, follow-up RX and worker processing time are different events and \mustnot\ replace that capture.

The association scope includes authenticated group, root identity/boot/source epoch, immediate sender identity/boot, train incarnation, capture epoch, publication, periodic event, channel and adapter/PHY context. Missing originals, ambiguous copies, replay, nonpreceding corrections and retired scope are refused. Counter wrap is valid only within its bounded live scope; overflow cannot alias an earlier event.

Let $M_g(x)$ map local counter $x$ to source time in immutable generation $g$. A completed pair supplies the anchor
\[
M_g(x_{\rm RX})=T_{\rm TX}+d,
\]
where $d$ is the declared path/reference treatment with its uncertainty. A new map becomes usable only after correction processing completes. Earlier reads or witness events retain the map then available; later evidence cannot improve past output.

\subsection*{Boundary clocks and paths}
An RC disciplines its own counter and originates a downstream train. Each correction uses the map effective at its own TX event and preserves that generation. It \mustnot\ present the parent's physical event as its own. Root identity, boot, source epoch, original issuance/expiry, UTC mapping and uncertainty remain provenance; sender boot, train and capture context describe the new transmitter.

RC7 authenticates a dynamic bounded path of root and at most two relay transmitters, including identities, boots, trains, capture epochs and slots. It rejects loops, incompatible scope and invalid parent/depth relationships. A leaf does not acquire relay or GM authority through admission. Root position zero and nominal RC positions at 15.625, 31.250 and 46.875 ms are scheduling policy, not PAwR response slots or proof of actual controller placement. Missed preparation advances by whole frames. Port-specific TX estimates require an explicit timing tier and uncertainty \cite{wire}.

\subsection*{Validity and continuity}
Functional availability, local discipline age, inherited age, independent source permission, admission/demand and precision quality are separate. T096 starts same-parent renewal at 240 s for direct and relay bindings, with at most a 360 s lease. The root source envelope is at most 1.65 s. Each consumer also applies its actual correction, fitter and profile expiry limits.

Holdover needs a declared model, elapsed age and supported error budget. The T096 RC's local BLE HOLD estimate has unknown bounds and no relay, USB observation, or source authority \cite{hold}. A receiver may keep acquiring the same authenticated direct train during a permission gap while withdrawing timing output; a context change requires fresh admission \cite{recovery}. Reboot, source/counter epoch change, revocation or arithmetic fault fences affected output. Within a continuous valid scope, delivered time is monotonic; reacquisition discontinuities are explicit.

GR3 correction slots start at 136 and 155, each 19 bytes: u32 publication, u16 event, u8 channel, u64 TX in 16 MHz ticks and u32 generation. RC7 slots start at 118 and 145, each 27 bytes: u32 publication, u16 event, u8 channel, u32 generation, u32 cumulative age in microseconds, u64 TX and u32 bound in nanoseconds. Correction count is at byte 7 in both; unused slots are zero. Valid precision bytes do not admit a source using an incompatible coarse revision.

The RC7 path MAC input is 89 bytes: u32 source epoch, u8 depth, and three 28-byte nodes. Each node is u64 ID, u64 boot, u32 train, u32 capture epoch, u8 slot, u8 role and two zero bytes. Unused nodes are all zero. The admitted path is required to verify the timing packet.

Flag masks are 1 ELIGIBLE, 2 SHADOW, 4 HOT, 8 YIELD and 16 CAPABLE. ANN and WITHDRAW must name the sending ACTIVE incarnation and carry full lineage. STATUS is the only kind that can set CAPABLE or echo HOT; a HOT echo identifies the sender's own boot and requires eligibility and shadow readiness. YIELD is valid only on ANN, with a named nominee and a stop boundary exactly 750 ms after the original UTC sample. WITHDRAW has zero flags. Maximum observation ages are 2.75 s for ANN, 4.5 s for STATUS and 1 s for WITHDRAW. Only ANN carries a positive source-valid field (at most 1.5 s); it does not grant GM receive permission.

\subsection*{ROLE and GNSS body}
Both kinds have zero header flags and no TZ field. Bytes 24--95 contain nine u64 values: sender, boot, sequence, sample monotonic microseconds, birth UTC microseconds, founder, term, ACTIVE ID and boot. Timing epoch and lifetime in microseconds are u32 at 96 and 100. ROLE then carries nominee ID/boot at 104--119, generations at 120--135, role/known/blockers at 136--139 and three sampled durations at 140--151. GNSS carries generations at 104--115, known/value masks at 116--119 and quality, ages and UTC error at 120--139. Observation limits are 4.5 s and 30 s, respectively; neither kind grants a timing map or HOT authority.

\section{Admission, receiver behavior and trust}
Receivers \must\ discover local meshes, keep trusted credentials separate from runtime mesh selection and authenticate admission afresh after context changes. A valid shared-key tag authenticates group membership and integrity. Any key holder can originate statements, so the tag provides no individual attestation or keyless verification. Payloads are readable on air.

Ordinary clients use direct JOIN or relay-local admission; GMs follow broadcast-selected timing. The T096 GATT transport writes complete records without response and requires an ATT MTU of at least 226 for group control. JOIN binds identities, boots, fresh nonces and forward sequences. The exchange state expires after 5 s; source-bearing extraction separately requires RTT below 250 ms. Coarse midpoint uncertainty includes half the RTT, the independent source bound and a drift/quantization margin. Remaining permission subtracts the full RTT plus those guards. The replay anchor \mustnot\ train the precision estimator. Relay admission binds the path, parent boot, challenge, nonce, publication floor and bounded source context \cite{wire}.

Demand and output availability are independent: an authenticated request can start acquisition while timing is unavailable. A not-ready response grants no timing. Renewing same-scope context preserves still-valid captures/maps when resources allow but \mustnot\ reset source age or resurrect expired output. Ordinary source advertisements and current usable source evidence, not a long demand lease, determine whether an ACTIVE GM can admit clients.

\subsection*{Receive procedure}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Reassemble a bounded whole record with transport and capture identity. Check exact length, discriminator, version/profile, kind and canonical reserved fields.
\item Verify group/key context and all applicable packet, origin and path tags. Bind the record to the selected sender, boot and admitted source/path scope before committing state.
\item Apply replay, sequence/publication floors, terminal/term fences and the original receipt/source deadlines. Reject foreign, duplicate, conflicting, expired or incompatible scope without renewing state.
\item Save a valid original's physical RX capture. Accept a later correction only for an unambiguous preceding original in that scope; check timestamp conversion and arithmetic bounds.
\item Publish a new immutable map only after processing completes. Report functional availability, source provenance, cumulative age and known or unknown uncertainty separately. Preserve refusals and unavailable opportunities.
\end{enumerate}
For ordinary GM7 broadcasts, original receipt time bounds local observation lifetime; polling, duplicate copies and delayed processing cannot extend it. Authentication and retained floors cannot prove the age of a previously unseen recording or advancing recorded stream. This first-contact replay limitation is explicit, including after eviction from bounded peer memory. Durable replay across arbitrary state loss, key rotation/revocation, compromise recovery, asymmetric public verification and delay-attack resistance need production security-profile work.

Capture callbacks use bounded work and storage. Report queue overflow without fabricating captures. Serving transitions require valid controller reservations, actual drain and guards against duplicate output. Ending diagnostic collection \mustnot\ stop healthy service. Explicit stop, fault containment and update drain remain bounded.

\section{Time zones and daylight-saving support}
The serving GM publishes one atomic pair in authenticated coarse metadata. TZ starts at GM body byte 162 (AD byte 166), JOIN byte 148, relay control byte 196 and relay capability byte 128. These are metadata offsets; the tag follows the four-byte pair.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{0.12\linewidth}L{0.24\linewidth}L{0.54\linewidth}@{}}\toprule
Bytes & Field & Meaning\\\midrule
0--1 & \code{tz\_id}, u16 LE & Stable append-only PToB identifier mapped to an IANA zone name.\\
2--3 & \code{tz\_offset\_min}, i16 LE & Current effective whole minutes east of UTC; valid range -840 to +840.\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
ID 0 is unknown, ID 1 is UTC (offset zero), and ID 2 is America/Chicago. The canonical unknown pair is \code{00 00 00 80}, representing (0, -32768). Chicago CDT is \code{02 00 d4 fe}, or (2, -300); CST is (2, -360). IDs identify regions, not seasonal abbreviations, and are PToB assignments rather than IANA numeric identifiers \cite{tz,zones}.

Receivers format local civil time as UTC plus offset$\times60$ seconds, with checked signed arithmetic. They use the transmitted effective offset and need no zone database or DST calculation. An unfamiliar nonzero ID with a valid offset remains usable and forwards unchanged. Invalid metadata normalizes to unknown without invalidating otherwise valid authenticated UTC; malformed framing or authentication failure rejects the packet. Unknown/expired metadata falls back to UTC.

\subsection*{Selection, ordering and propagation}
The GM may use automatic location-based selection, a manually selected zone/calendar, or an explicit fixed offset. The reference automatic selector uses its own fresh GNSS fixes, conservative region coverage and a finite calendar validity window. Ambiguous/stale location permits only the declared bounded hold; an expired calendar produces unknown immediately. Standby GMs may prepare their own zone; on takeover the new ACTIVE publishes its own selected pair, including unknown if unavailable. Geographic coverage and calendar support are implementation capabilities, not universal protocol guarantees \cite{auto}.

An RC learns the pair only from its selected authenticated parent and forwards it unchanged in coarse capability/admission records. Direct updates require ordered selected-GM ANN with current root/boot/epoch scope and replay checks. Initial JOIN metadata cannot overwrite a later accepted ANN. Relay changes use the existing monotonic admission challenge: a higher token can change the pair; an equal token renews only an identical pair. Older or equal-token conflicting updates are refused. No TZ counter or transition schedule is added.

TZ has a local presence lease of three nominal parent advertising periods: 750 ms for a 250 ms GM cadence, 300 ms for a 100 ms RC cadence. Matching authenticated parent advertisements may renew that lease independently of precision corrections, lock and map expiry. Each hop renews its own presence lease. Polling cannot renew it. After expiry the live value is unknown; the last accepted pair may support matching-presence renewal. An explicit unknown update clears that pair, and parent/path replacement fences the old context.

Receivers must continue receiving coarse updates after timing synchronization. Precision presence can renew a cached pair but carries no changed pair. A DST transition changes the effective offset on fresh coarse reception; simultaneous scheduled display changes are not promised. Repeated local hours retain distinct UTC event identities.

TZ \mustnot\ alter precision G1/GR3/RC7 payloads, UTC mappings, clock discipline, PPS, source/mesh epochs, replay comparisons, USB UTC or chrony inputs. The precision field \code{utc\_offset\_ns} maps source coordinates to UTC and \mustnot\ be repurposed for civil-time offset. TZ grants no timing permission. Other bearers require their own explicit metadata contract.

\section{Conformance targets and proposed SIG work}
Conformance is layered: byte interoperability, state/authority correctness, radio-event association, functional availability and independently measured precision require separate results. No codec, model, native build, fitted residual or successful radio request establishes all of them.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{0.20\linewidth}L{0.72\linewidth}@{}}\toprule
Area & Required verification\\\midrule
Wire and bearers & Exact versions/lengths/tags, canonical padding, complete reassembly, malformed/refused vectors, transport/discovery binding and cross-vendor decoding.\\
GM7 authority & Independent qualification; initial election; actual HOT confirmation; source/control loss; immutable YIELD/WITHDRAW; physical stop-before-start; conditional 3 s first-G1 target; no-HOT fallback; partition merge.\\
Timing and relays & Unique TX/RX originals and corrections; capture/scope transitions, replay and wrap; causal map activation; original expiry; one- and two-relay paths; actual controller placement and loss recovery.\\
TZ and DST & Signed/fractional-hour offsets, unknown/future IDs, ordered direct/relay updates, withdrawal and exact lease expiry, parent/GM replacement, repeated/skipped local hours and unchanged UTC/precision bytes.\\
Precision and resources & Named output/reference points, calibration and uncertainty, bias and total RMS, tails, unavailable/missing counts, drift/holdover, coexistence, energy and sustained operation.\\
Security and lifecycle & Current admission, key/epoch/boot changes, bounded inventories, compromise/revocation and replay limits, diagnostic independence, explicit stop and fault/resource closure.\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}

The edition manifest binds the source snapshot, wire fixtures and offline checks. In the October 29 T096 0.15.209 campaign, all three direct receivers showed the correct route and a valid locked map in 116/116 samples each. In a separate 600 s relay window, one leaf lost its route and map in 20/116 samples; the route failed the 99\% gate \cite{auto}. These windows do not qualify this snapshot or the HOT path. Physical failover, sustained relay availability, cross-vendor interoperability, constrained listening and calibrated UTC/output bounds remain open. Report denominators, invalid/missing observations and failures; keep internal fit, relative PPS and independent output measurements separate.

The proposed SIG work item is a passive clock-service/profile family with explicit event association, causal publication, authenticated authority separation and interoperable quality reporting. The production profile must settle assigned identifiers, discoverable service/capability definitions, connected-record fragmentation/MTU behavior, controller timestamp/scheduling support and security lifecycle. Claimed listening or energy tiers need measured acquisition, recovery and steady-service budgets.

Before public review, check the G7V4 field and tag boundaries against a second decoder, confirm the distinction between liveness and timing permission in each takeover case, and confirm that the open qualification items are stated as such. This profile uses Bluetooth periodic advertising as a bearer; it is not an adopted Bluetooth SIG profile or IEEE PTP/gPTP conformance claim.
