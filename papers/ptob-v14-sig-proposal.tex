\begin{abstract}
Passive Precision Time over BLE (PToB) proposes a common time service for heterogeneous listeners: clocks, sensor event recorders and devices with different capture, computation and power capabilities. V14 distributes complete periodic timing records, with corrections for actual earlier physical events, so steady reception needs no per-timestamp transaction. Independently sourced grandmasters coordinate directly; dedicated relay clocks discipline a local counter and regenerate downstream timing with immutable causal map generations and preserved root provenance. This proposal specifies profiles, lifecycle, authentication, original-event matching, exact portable byte layouts, rejection rules and conformance targets for the frozen V14-RC1 candidate. Public reception and trusted clock acceptance are distinct: the current group-key profile provides symmetric authentication, while keyless public verification is future work. A prospective physical one-relay experiment supports the boundary-clock mechanism and is summarized in a separate evidence annex. Numerical accuracy, constrained listening and standards qualification remain properties requiring named implementation and measurement evidence.
\end{abstract}
\section{Service purpose and document status}
The universal service lets an endpoint learn a source coordinate, maintain a usable local clock between observations, identify its provenance and withdraw stale time explicitly. A callback-only endpoint may offer coarse interpretation; a receiver with uniquely associated physical captures may refine synchronization. The packet resolution does not impose the best prototype accuracy on every listener.

This is a completed local candidate specification/profile proposal for discussion, not a submission, adopted SIG specification or qualified product. \must, \mustnot\ and \should\ describe the candidate's requirements. V14-RC1 at revision \code{7995249} is the frozen target \cite{rc1}; this document transcribes its bytes and semantics rather than designing a new on-air version. The portable wire annex and synthetic examples are normative within that target. The evidence annex is nonnormative. Open dispositions distinguish target requirements from codec enforcement and unfinished production profile work.

The 2 October proposal remains a historical compatibility reference. Its 31-byte multipart services and compact control cannot be silently relabeled as V14. The separate 12 October V14 research preprint analyzes implementation and measurements; this document instead supplies an implementable service contract and review agenda.

\section{Profiles, roles and authority}
The service separates capabilities, configured profile, admission, election, actual serving and precision quality. A supported profile is not an elected role. A good fitted clock does not create an independent source.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{0.2\linewidth}L{0.72\linewidth}@{}}\toprule
Profile / role & Required function and authority\\\midrule
GM source profile & Independently eligible source; directly admitted and elected before originating source time.\\
Coordinator & Manages authenticated GM participants; coordination alone conveys no timing precision.\\
Standby / HOT & Own eligible source plus synchronization to selected GM. HOT additionally needs actual synchronization and current takeover authorization.\\
Dedicated RC & One authenticated parent, local counter discipline and downstream regenerated timing. No GM, coordinator or standby entitlement.\\
Client / leaf & Consumes one admitted GM or RC train. A relay-only leaf obeys its declared parent constraint; it originates no timing/control authority.\\
Constrained listener & Separate optional profile with complete acquisition, recovery and shutdown inside at most 50\% listening. Evidence remains pending.\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}

An authority-bearing profile transition \must\ fence the old source/control/timing scope before new admission. Dedicated RC service cannot become GM or HOT through renewed demand, held time, good fit RMS or a source-looking UI. A device supporting several profiles still requires an explicit selected policy; RC1 does not promise live profile switching.

GM control includes at most four participants, coordinator included. Each remote participant \must\ maintain a direct authenticated coordinator connection. Relay-only or overflow candidates become clients; relay paths \mustnot\ carry GM management. This cap does not limit ordinary client identities, route-table identities or public listeners.

The service's ``boundary clock'' describes regeneration through a disciplined local clock. IEEE 1588 boundary-clock terminology motivates that distinction \cite{nist}; PToB does not claim PTP message, port, media, BMCA or IEEE 802.1AS profile conformance. A wired gateway needs its own time-scale, delay and uncertainty contract.

\section{Transport, registry and compatibility}
Core timing uses LE periodic advertising without responses, with a 62.5 ms frame (16 Hz). Root slot zero and RC nominal positions 15.625, 31.250 and 46.875 ms are protocol profile positions. They are neither PAwR response slots nor a guarantee that a generic controller API can phase-lock independent trains.

Bluetooth discovery uses extended advertising: ADV\_EXT\_IND points to AUX\_ADV\_IND carrying SyncInfo; the periodic train carries AUX\_SYNC\_IND and any subordinate data. Synchronization is distinct from connecting. The controller owns scheduling and channel selection; SyncInfo describes train acquisition, not UTC \cite{core}. Complete means a complete application AD record, not an assumption of one HCI report or one unfragmented RF PDU.

The precision records use AD type \code{0xff} (Manufacturer Specific Data), whose initial company-code bytes follow the SIG data-type definition \cite{css,assigned}. RC1 uses experimental \code{0xffff}, not an assigned production company identifier or service UUID. Connected JOIN/GM control uses a separate connection; no GATT service/characteristic identifier or universal fragmentation binding is assigned here.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{0.24\linewidth}L{0.68\linewidth}@{}}\toprule

A 31-byte-only legacy listener cannot consume a 190-byte V14 record. Keep legacy services separate if offered, with explicit resource budgets; they supply no V14 authority. Older G1/GR1/GR2, RC/RQ profile revisions, JOIN3 and earlier control types are rejected by the selected V14 decoder. Migration fences pending captures, replay floors, maps and old authority, then authenticates the new profile. Version rejection is safer than inferring equivalence from equal sizes. Production identifiers and connected-bearer interoperability are open profile tasks.

\section{Independent-source election and partition convergence}
Eligibility \must\ be evaluated before deterministic ranking. A candidate needs actual independently eligible source/time evidence and authorized direct GM participation. Unknown quality cannot be improved by numerical defaults, a device name, uptime, fitted residuals or relay holdover. The rank tuple is lexicographic, smaller preferred:
\[
(\mathrm{priority1},\mathrm{class},\mathrm{accuracy},\mathrm{variance},\mathrm{priority2},\mathrm{stable\ identity}).
\]
The preferred eligible candidate is GM; with a second eligible candidate, that candidate supplies the coordinator/standby assignment. HOT is a further synchronized and permitted state, not a synonym for coordinator. Singleton operation requires an explicit local management path. Stable identities, not names or fixed hardware roles, break ties.

Startup \must\ include at least 60 s of receive observation and 60 s of stable eligible-source evidence. These waits may overlap. Source-epoch change, expiry, receive fault or invalid local time resets affected admission. Missing reception cannot be counted as evidence of isolation. A forced-role request cannot create source readiness.

Each authenticated connected partition chooses one eligible GM. Disconnected partitions may legitimately serve from different independent sources. Merge, accepted source change, reboot and withdrawal \must\ invalidate old live authority/timing scope before replacement. This is partition-local convergence, not global consensus across absent links. Retained old time, where explicitly permitted, is HOLD without current root entitlement.

The takeover defaults begin suspicion after 2 s without fresh authenticated root health and retain a 3 s local source-change fence before replacement serving. Actual eligibility, direct connectivity and scheduling still apply; this is not a guaranteed 5 s recovery bound. Loss of a consumer's map alone is not proof of root failure.

JOIN authentication establishes identities and a transient connection context; it does not admit a fifth GM or declare precision. GC SERVICE, HELLO and PREPARE/READY/COMMIT statements carry source and management scope under current permission. Decision/sequence, boot, challenge and current source evidence must be checked before those statements affect roles. A coordinator's lease or a standby-pair hint is not new independent-source evidence.

\section{Boundary regeneration, routes and causal timing}
An RC disciplines a local monotonic counter from completed parent TX/RX event pairs and independently originates a downstream train. Each outgoing correction uses the map effective at its own captured TX event and preserves that immutable generation. It \mustnot\ forward a parent packet as though the parent physically emitted the RC's event. Transparent residence correction is a separate unimplemented profile.

The fixed route table is authenticated, acyclic and at most eight entries. Root entry zero transmits in slot zero. A transmitting child has a prior table entry as parent, depth parent+1 (maximum two), a distinct slot 1--3 later than its parent's slot, and a transmitting parent. A leaf has slot 255, no TX and its parent's depth. Routes are configured policy, not a dynamic mesh/slot allocator. Live addresses, boot identities and train/capture/source scopes must still be discovered and authenticated. Slot ownership is not source authority.

A conceptual physical-copy key includes admitted group and route, selected source/root boot and epoch, sender identity and boot, train incarnation, capture epoch, publication, periodic event, physical channel and capture adapter/PHY context. The original carries no prediction of its own future TX. Later packets carry zero, one or two corrections naming earlier publications/events/channels. Receivers \must\ match the saved physical RX capture of that original; the correction packet's own channel, callback or worker time cannot replace it.

For local counter $x$, let $M_g(x)$ be the causal map in generation $g$. A corrected original supplies the anchor $M_g(x_{\rm RX})=T_{\rm TX}+d$, where $d$ is declared propagation/reference treatment with its uncertainty. The new map takes effect no earlier than usable correction availability $a$. A read before $a$ uses an older map or is unavailable; later evidence cannot improve earlier output.

An RC preserves root identity/boot/source epoch, original issuance/expiry, UTC offset and frame provenance while reporting its own sender boot/train/publication/capture context and per-correction cumulative age. Authenticate GR3 before converting $q$ wire ticks to $125q/2$ ns with checked integer/rational arithmetic. The pinned adapter floors a half-nanosecond; another adapter must declare its quantization treatment. Translate the root values into RC5's distinct layout and domain. Re-signing them creates a symmetric provenance statement, not a new GM observation or a publicly verifiable root signature.

The two-RC path is the target capability. One physical RC, a two-RC simulator and three available RC positions are separate facts. Missed preparation positions advance by whole frames; actual supported controller placement and unique TX/RX association require physical evidence.

\section{Trust, membership and local service lifecycle}
The current trust profile uses a provisioned 32-byte shared group PSK, group identity and group epoch. Every key holder can generate group-authenticated statements. HMAC-SHA256-128 establishes a symmetric trust boundary, not individual hardware attestation, secrecy of broadcast time or keyless public verification \cite{hmac}. Public listeners can receive/parse timing without a connection; accepting it as trusted clock discipline needs the selected authenticated context.

Fresh challenges, sender/receiver boots, nonces, forward sequences and publication floors bind admission and fence replay. Connection address or display name alone is insufficient. Reconnection requires fresh proof even when membership metadata remains. Cold bootstrap establishes a bounded coarse replay/freshness interval only: it \mustnot\ train the precision estimator, confer source candidacy or renew original source permission.

JOIN4 uses REQUEST, CHALLENGE, PROOF and ACCEPT (or BUSY). The exchange lifetime is at most 5 s. Coarse extraction requires causal RTT at most 250 ms and independently stated source-map uncertainty. Source-bearing records have bounded remaining time, not perpetual validity. Control participation and local downstream demand are independent flags/states.

RQ5 capability advertises a next challenge. A child REQUEST and parent's RESPONSE bind route indices, boots, challenge, nonce and sequence. An authenticated request may start RC acquisition without a usable map. RESPONSE ready=0 means no timing admission; demand may exist while timing is unavailable. ready=1 binds parent train/capture epoch, publication floor, root scope, coarse interval and admission expiry. An admitted demand is not proof of delivered output.

Fixed-route service renews approximately every 300 s with at most 360 s local lease/context and a bounded 2 s transaction. Upstream demand must conservatively reflect live downstream demand. Renewal preserves still-valid same-scope maps, captures and sync when controller resources allow; it cannot reset clock age, source evidence or an expired map. Changed timebase/source scope fences affected state. A recreated train clears pending associations while any explicitly retained same-timebase map keeps its original expiry.

Replay refusal, demand expiry, timing age and source-serving permission must remain separately observable. RC1 does not specify production enrollment, compromise recovery, asymmetric public signatures, arbitrary-state-loss durable replay or delay-attack resistance. These are external security/profile requirements, not capabilities implied by a correct MAC.

\section{Clock validity, age and resource safety}
Functional states are UNAVAILABLE, TRACKING and HELD (reference interfaces may name TRACKING ``LOCKED''). Precision quality is separate: UNKNOWN/UNQUALIFIED or a supported conservative bound. Lock or PLL activity is not accuracy qualification.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{0.2\linewidth}L{0.72\linewidth}@{}}\toprule
Quantity & Required meaning / limit\\\midrule
Local discipline age & Elapsed since last accepted discipline became usable. Fixed-route RC/leaf output expires at age $\ge500$ ms.\\
Inherited map age & Parent cumulative age plus local elapsed time; new local correction cannot erase it.\\
Root evidence / permission & Original provenance and live independent source are distinct. GR3 source-serving permission is at most 1.65 s.\\
Admission / demand & At most 360 s local context; approximately 300 s renewal. RC5 representation is not 360 s fresh GM permission.\\
Legacy windowed leaf & Separate 1.5 s reference policy clipped by original permission; never combined with the fixed-route rule.\\
Hold precision metadata & Horizon/budget zero together means no bounded precision grant. Nonzero values require independent bounds; do not override functional expiry.\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}

At exactly 500 ms after the last accepted update, fixed-route time is unavailable. A child may receive a newly accepted correction from a held RC and renew its local lifetime, but inherited age grows. Thus 500 ms is a local operating policy, not an oscillator error bound or cumulative-chain guarantee. Rejected traffic, renewals, retransmissions and reads never renew discipline age.

Source/counter epoch change, reboot, revocation, overflow or timebase fault fences maps. Within a continuous valid scope, delivered time is monotonic. Unqualified reacquisition steps must be explicitly fenced/reported; expiry invalidates time, current fit/lock and precision claims while discovery continues. Unknown capture, rate, path and reference uncertainty propagate as unknown, not zero.

Healthy installed normal service \must\ autostart and keep running after diagnostics end. Finite capture stops logging only; it adds no service/RF shutdown timer. Actual explicit stop, update drain, fault containment and demand expiry retain bounded closure. Bounded queues/histories refuse overflow; scope exhaustion fences/drains before reuse. Capture callbacks avoid unbounded work, formatting, allocation or flash writes. Supported controller ownership and undrained transmitter faults remain hard resource gates.

LoRa, WiFi and PTS require separate profiles/evidence; this proposal activates none. The separate dual-bearer precision gate remains a total held delivered-time bound within 500 ns across actual blackout plus margins, not a percentile or fit-RMS screen.

\section{Wire conventions and authentication coverage}
All offsets are zero-based within the complete record, excluding link-layer headers. All multibyte integers are unsigned little-endian unless stated signed; signed UTC offset is 64-bit two's complement. Byte arrays (names, magic and tags) retain their byte order. The GM 16 MHz representation is a 62.5 ns wire unit; it does not require a 16 MHz physical counter or imply that accuracy. Nanosecond RC fields likewise specify representation, not precision. Field width is the byte count in the tables. HMAC tags are the first 16 digest bytes, not integers.

The group context $C$ is 13 bytes: context-version byte 1, group ID (u64 LE), group epoch (u32 LE). For each eight-byte ASCII domain $D$ and body $B$:
\[\mathrm{tag}_{128}=\mathrm{first}_{16}(\mathrm{HMAC\!\!\!-SHA256}(K,D\Vert C\Vert B)).\]
Route digest is the first eight bytes under RC5ROUTE over the complete 97-byte table. Ranges below use an exclusive upper bound.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{0.32\linewidth}L{0.6\linewidth}@{}}\toprule
Tag/domain & Authenticated body and tag location\\\midrule
GR3 origin: PTOBG3O3 & wire[14:74], 60 bytes; tag at 74--89.\\
GR3 packet: PTOBG3R3 & wire[0:174]; tag at 174--189.\\
RC5 origin: RC5ORIGN & route digest (8 bytes) followed by wire[12:72]; tag at 72--87.\\
RC5 packet: RC5PACKT & wire[0:174]; tag at 174--189.\\
RQ5: RC5LOCAL & wire[0:120]; tag at 120--135.\\
JOIN4: PTOBG4J4 & wire[0:148]; tag at 148--163.\\
GC17: PTOBG3C4 & wire[0:152], or HELLO wire[0:207]; final 16 bytes are tag.\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}

\textbf{Frozen notation disposition I-01.} The contract's GR3 origin range is [14,60]. The frozen vectors and immutable reference C authenticate 60 bytes starting at 14, i.e. [14:74]. This proposal states start/length explicitly; it does not change the contract. Reading 60 as an exclusive endpoint would omit frame/offset bytes and break the known answers.

Length, magic/type, exact version/profile, epoch, both applicable tags, route, reserved zeros and semantic constraints must pass before discipline or lease renewal. Correction count is 0--2; every unused entry is all zero. UNKNOWN u32 uncertainty is \code{0xffffffff}. Hold-horizon/budget are authenticated unsigned quantities (``signed policy'' means MAC protected, not negative integers). UTF-8 names have explicit byte length and zero padding, never authorization by name.

Required identities/incarnations must be nonzero except documented null fields; event 0 is a valid u16 periodic counter value. Timing channels are 0--36 (secondary/data channels), not the historical primary-channel 37--39 target codes. Overflow/wrap must not alias a live physical copy or scope.

\textbf{Implementation gap I-02.} The pinned GR3 semantic validator checks timestamp/generation/ranges but does not itself enforce every RC1 nonpreceding/duplicate-publication rule. The proposal retains those required checks at the receiver pipeline; their transition-complete implementation validation remains open. RC5's validator explicitly checks preceding publications and duplicate tuples. The route-MAC body is one count byte (2--8), then eight entries of 12 bytes: identity u64 LE, parent u8, slot u8, depth u8, transmitter u8 (0/1), in that order. Unused entries are all zero.
