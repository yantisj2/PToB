# Research evidence

Imports are pending. Start with the claim index once published. Each package
will identify its run, method, implementation, population, outcome, limitations,
analysis commands, and checksums.

Model output, controller simulation, hardware observations, and independently
measured output remain separate. Review status is recorded independently of
evidence type. Failed and incomplete runs relevant to a claim remain visible.

Publication datasets are curated derivatives of preserved originals. Redactions
and transformations are documented. Larger archives may be hosted separately
with durable references and checksums; missing public data limits reproducibility.
